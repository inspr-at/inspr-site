An ultra-detailed overhead macro photograph for a precision-objects catalogue. Same artist, same series as the inspr.at v2 portfolio.

Composition: an overhead photograph, flat-on, of four small precision-machined geometric solids laid out in a horizontal row, evenly spaced on a deeper cream archival surface. From left to right the four solids are: (1) a thin cream-coloured cylinder, end-on; (2) a small cube; (3) a flat disc; (4) a low triangular prism. Each solid is hand-cut from cream-warm-toned ceramic or polished bone-coloured stone. On the top face of each solid is a single small distinct abstract calligraphic ink gesture — NOT a letter, NOT a number, just a small abstract brushstroke shape. Each gesture is different from the others but share the same brush style. The solids cast soft, calibrated shadows on the surface beneath them.

Palette: warm cream archival surface (#F2EDDE), slightly cooler cream stone solids, deep warm ink-near-black (#1A1714) for the four ink gestures. NO other colours.

Light: even diffused overhead studio light, soft shadows under each solid.

Aesthetic: a contemporary precision-objects museum display, photographed for an architecture monograph.

Hard exclusions: NO text, NO numerals, NO recognisable letters or symbols, NO arrows, NO logos. The marks on the solids are ABSTRACT brushstrokes ONLY. NO bright colours. NO figures.

Aspect ratio: 16:9, cinematic.
