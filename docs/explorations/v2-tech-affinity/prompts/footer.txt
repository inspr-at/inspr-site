An ultra-detailed overhead macro photograph for a precision-printing catalogue. Same artist, same series as the inspr.at v2 portfolio.

Composition: an overhead flat-on photograph of a single small abstract precision register mark on a cream archival paper card. The card occupies the right third of the frame; the rest of the frame is the deeper cream surface (the table). The register mark consists of two delicate perpendicular lines — one horizontal, one vertical — crossing precisely at right angles, with a single small dot at the intersection. The lines are equal length, ~2 cm each, hand-printed in deep warm ink-near-black. The card has gentle aged edges and a slight visible age-tone toward the corners.

Palette: warm cream paper card, slightly deeper cream surface beneath, deep warm ink-near-black for the register mark. NO other colours.

Light: low directional from the upper-left, soft, raking across the card to show its slight curvature and texture.

Aesthetic: a printer's register mark from a fine-press letterpress proof — quiet, precise, intimate.

Hard exclusions: NO text, NO numerals, NO recognisable letters, NO logos. The register mark is two perpendicular lines and a centre dot ONLY. NO figures.

Aspect ratio: 21:9 panoramic, ultrawide.
