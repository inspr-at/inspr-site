An ultra-detailed macro photograph for the cover of a materials-science monograph. Same artist, same series as the inspr.at v2 portfolio.

Composition: a polished cross-section of a precision-engineered laminate composite material, photographed flat-on, filling the frame. The composite is built from horizontal layers of slightly different cream-warm-grey tones — each layer between 1 mm and 3 mm thick, with subtle internal structure visible at high magnification. The layers form a clean horizontal stratification across the entire frame. Some layers are denser (darker), some are lighter; the overall composition has a calm rhythm of tonal bands.

Palette: warm cream (#E8E2D6) for the lightest bands, through warm taupe and soft umber, to deep warm ink-near-black (#1A1714) for the darkest bands. All warm-toned. NO bright colours, NO blues, NO greens, NO saturated hues.

Light: calibrated raking light from the upper-left, low angle, brings out the surface micro-relief of each layer.

Aesthetic: an electron-microscope image of a precision-engineered laminate, but in visible light. Materials-science publication quality. Hasselblad H6D-100c, Schneider 120mm macro at f/11, large-format archival print quality.

Hard exclusions: NO text, NO numerals, NO measurement scales, NO ruler marks, NO labels, NO recognisable letters or symbols, NO logos. NO figures, NO faces, NO hands. NO bright colours. NO digital painting look.

Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical, portrait orientation. The image is a TALL VERTICAL companion strip.
