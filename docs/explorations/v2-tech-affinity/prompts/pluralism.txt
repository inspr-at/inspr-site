An ultra-detailed macro photograph for a precision-engineering catalogue. Same artist, same series as the inspr.at v2 portfolio.

Composition: a polished cream-warm-coloured ceramic or stone slab, photographed flat-on from directly above, filling the frame. Etched into the surface are precision-machined channels: three parallel thin channels enter from the left edge of the frame, converge to a single small circular well at the exact centre, then three new channels diverge from that well to the right edge of the frame. Each channel is about 1 mm wide and 0.5 mm deep, with crisp tooled edges. The channels lie on a strict horizontal axis with the well at dead centre. The three left-side channels are NOT parallel — they angle inward to converge. The three right-side channels are NOT parallel — they fan outward.

Palette: warm cream (#E8E2D6) for the slab, deep warm ink-near-black (#1A1714) inside the channels (where shadow falls into the etched depth). NO other colours.

Light: calibrated overhead studio light, slightly raked, brings out the depth of each channel as a clean dark line.

Aesthetic: contemporary industrial design. The slab looks like a piece of architectural sculpture from a Donald Judd retrospective, but engineered to micrometre precision.

Hard exclusions: NO text, NO numerals, NO arrows, NO arrowheads, NO measurement marks, NO labels, NO recognisable letters or symbols, NO logos. The channels must read as ABSTRACT precision-engineered grooves only. NO bright colours. NO figures.

Aspect ratio: 16:9, cinematic.
